- A new launch animation: the Y and M fly in and lock together to make the logo.
- Film and series pages, Netflix-style: Resume with its progress, Play From Beginning and Episodes & More, or Play and Audio & Subtitles for something new. Something you're part way through plays behind the page.
- Home: rest on a film or series and it widens and plays a preview. Turn it off in Settings → Film & Series Previews.
- The player, Netflix-style: ▲ for Back, Play From Beginning, Next Episode and Episodes; ▼ for Settings. The title shows at the top right while paused, and holding OK opens the menu straight away.
- Skip Intro and Skip Recap now also use SkipDB and IntroDB when TheIntroDB has nothing. The player's settings show what was found for each episode.
- Search has its own keyboard, like Netflix, so the TV's keyboard no longer pops up.
- Because you watched: only titles you actually watched, and hold OK on a card for "Don't recommend based on this".
- Picture: choose Fit, Fill screen, Stretch, 16:9 or 4:3 in the player's settings, remembered for each channel, film and series.
- Smoother, Netflix-paced animations, and no more cut-off highlights.
