- Sync with your other devices: favourites, Continue Watching, watched, your queue, recently watched channels, favourite, hidden and renamed categories, picture shape, subtitle style and languages and your Home rows now follow you between your Fire TV, phone, Mac and Samsung TV when they use the same login, usually within seconds. It's encrypted on the device, so the sync server can't see your login or what you watch. Settings → Sync to turn it off or sync now.
- Film and series pages show the title's logo, like Netflix. On a series it shrinks out of the way when the seasons come up.
- Recent Channels: take a channel off the list one at a time (☰ or hold OK on it, on Home or in Live TV → Recent Channels). It comes off your other devices too.
- Home: the film or series you're on widens straight away, like Netflix, showing its picture and logo, and starts playing a preview almost at once. Move along and the next one widens straight away.
- A new launch intro: "YOUSUF MIAH" folds away into its Y and M, which land in the logo.
- Home, like Netflix: bigger posters, and under the one you're on: series or film, genre, year, seasons or running time, the UK age rating and a summary.
- Continue Watching: stop an episode in its last 3 minutes (the credits) and it counts as watched, with the next episode waiting in Continue Watching, even across seasons.
