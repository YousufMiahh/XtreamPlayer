- Picture: press ☰ (or hold OK) while watching and choose Fit, Fill screen, Stretch, 16:9 or 4:3. It's remembered for each channel, film and series.
- "Because you watched" now only uses films and series you actually watched (finished, or at least 5 minutes in), not ones you only opened or started by accident.
- Skip Recap: alongside Skip Intro, from TheIntroDB. The player's Audio & Subtitles panel shows what it found for each episode.
- The player, Netflix-style: press ▲ for Back, Play From Beginning, Next Episode, Episodes and Settings. The title shows at the top right while paused. Hold OK now opens the menu straight away.
- Film and series pages, Netflix-style: Resume with its progress, Play From Beginning and Episodes & More, or Play and Audio & Subtitles for something new. Something you're part way through plays behind the page.
- Home: rest on a film or series and it widens and plays a preview. Turn it off in Settings → Film & Series Previews.
- Because you watched: hold OK on a card for "Don't recommend based on this".
